# How Cojaunt works

A simple guide for members, the team and anyone new to the project.

Cojaunt helps you do one of three things:

| You want to… | Go to | What you send |
|---|---|---|
| **Date** someone | Discover → **Date** | a **Spark** to that person |
| Travel with **one person** | Discover → **Travel buddy** | a **travel request** to that person |
| Join a **hosted trip** | Discover → **Trips** | a **request to join**, to the host |

These three never mix. Dating is about people, travel buddies are about people, and trips are about groups.

There are **two kinds of trip**, and each has its own list:

| | Group trip | Singles trip |
|---|---|---|
| Who can see and join | Everyone | Only single members who chose **Open to dating** |
| Who's on it | Anyone, women & men, or women-only / men-only | Singles only, always mixed, one age band (max 12 years) |
| Seats per request | Up to 4 (bring friends) | 1 (you come on your own) |
| Where to find it | Trips → **Group trips** | Trips → **Singles trips** |

You always choose. Nobody can message you unless you said yes.

## 1. Sign up (about 3 minutes)

0. **Create an account** (`/register`): mobile number, email and password. Tick that you are 18+ and accept the Terms and Community Guidelines, and consent to the Privacy Policy. Marketing email is a separate, optional tick. A 6-digit code confirms your mobile number. Already a member? **Sign in** with your email or mobile. Forgot your password? Reset it with a code. **Sign out** is in the avatar menu and on your profile.
1. **Basics**: first name, date of birth (18+ only), gender, single or married.
2. **Photos (required)**: add **at least 1** and up to 6 photos of yourself, then do a quick **selfie check**. You can't continue without a photo, so every member others see has a real picture. Later, on your profile, your last photo can't be removed (add a new one first to replace it).
3. **Travel style**: six quick questions (pace, budget, early bird or night owl…).
4. **Interests**, and whether you're here for **new friends** or **open to dating**. Only you can see this choice.
5. **Privacy** settings. Safe defaults are already on.

## 2. Discover: pick what you want

Discover has three big buttons at the top: **Date · Travel buddy · Trips**. All cards in a list are the same height, so they line up neatly.

### Your location (works anywhere in India)
- The first time you open Discover, the app asks for your location **once**.
- If you say yes, your location shows **in the top bar on every page**, for example **"📍 Bhiwandi · 50 km"** (phones show just the city). Tap it, or hover on a computer, to see the full place, such as "Kaneri, Bhiwandi, Maharashtra".
- People and trips are then sorted **nearest first**. Each person shows their **city** and a rough distance ("Thane · 5–15 km away"), and each trip shows how far it starts from you ("Pickup 18 km from you · Thane").
- Tap the location in the top bar to **change** it:
  - **Use my current location** again
  - **Choose a city** from the list (grouped by state, from Agra to Visakhapatnam)
  - **How far**: 10, 25, 50 or 200 km, or anywhere in India
  - **Don't use my location**
- **When you search for a trip, location is set aside** and all of India is searched ("Searching all of India" appears above the results). Clear the search and it goes back to nearest first.
- Notices such as "2 people want to connect" and "How was your trip?" sit **side by side** at the top of Discover (stacked on phones). Tap one to go straight there.
- If you say no, the place name can't be found, or you're outside India, everything still works: you can choose a city, or browse all of India.
- **Privacy:** your location is rounded to about 1 km before it's used or sent, is forgotten when you close the tab, and is never shown to other members. They see only your **city**. Distances to people are rough bands, never exact numbers.

### Date
- Shows **single people who are open to dating**, and only people. Married members and people who chose "New friends" never appear here.
- Tap a person to open their **profile** and send a **Spark**.
- Trips for singles are **not** shown here. They're under **Trips → Singles trips**, so dating and trips never get mixed up.

### Travel buddy
- Shows **verified people** you could travel with one-to-one.
- People who like the same things as you appear first.
- You can switch to **Women only** or **Men only** if you'd prefer a companion of your own gender.

### Trips
- At the top, a switch: **Group trips | Singles trips**. Each shows only its own kind.
  - Members who aren't open to dating (or are married) see **Group trips only**; the switch is hidden.
- Each card shows whether it's a **day plan** or an **overnight trip**, the date, the place, the distance from you, the price and seats left.
- Search ("slow trek under 5k") and filters ("This weekend", "Only women") narrow the list.
- Tap a card, then **Request to join** (see section 5).

## 3. Opening someone's profile

Anyone can open anyone's profile. You'll see:

| Everyone can see | Hidden until a request is accepted |
|---|---|
| Photos (exactly as many as they uploaded) | Surname |
| First name and age | Where they work |
| What they do, in a few words | Which area they live in |
| About, interests, travel style | Instagram handle |
| Verified badge and plans done | |

Use the arrows or thumbnails to look through their photos. The counter (for example **2 / 5**) shows how many photos they have.

## 4. Sending a Spark or a travel request

On a profile, at most two buttons appear:

- **Spark** (pink): "I'd like to get to know you, maybe date". It only appears if you're both single and open to dating.
- **Travel together** (green): "Let's be travel buddies".

Then:

1. Write a short note if you like (up to 200 characters).
2. Tap **Send**.
3. The other person sees your request under **Requests → Received**.
4. They choose **Accept** or **Not now**.

### What happens next

| They tap… | What you see | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Accept** | "Accepted" | A **private chat** opens, and you can **both see each other's private details** |
| **Not now** | "Not this time" | Nothing else. They aren't told anything more, and **you can't ask again** |
| Nothing yet | "Waiting for a reply" | You can **Withdraw** at any time |

## 5. Joining a trip

1. Discover → **Trips** → pick **Group trips** or **Singles trips** → tap a card.
2. Read who's going (first names show only after you're approved), the plan, price and host.
3. Tap **Request to join** and add a short note for the host.
4. The **host approves** you.
5. **Pay** to confirm your seat. Your money is held until the plan happens.
6. You're in. The **group chat** and **Trip Mode** (SOS, check-ins, sharing with family) open.

After the plan, you can privately mark people from the group as **Travel again** or **Spark**. If someone chose you too, you're connected.

## 6. Unfriend, mute and block

You stay in control of who can reach you. Use the **"…"** button on anyone's profile (or in a private chat):

| Option | When to use it | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Mute** (unfollow) | Someone is irritating but not harmful | They disappear from your Discover, and their requests stop notifying you. You can still open their profile. Unmute any time from the "…" menu or the **Safety centre** |
| **Unfriend** | You no longer want to be connected | Your private chat closes, your private details are hidden from each other again, and they leave **Connected**. Either of you can send a new request later |
| **Block** | Someone makes you uncomfortable | You disappear for each other everywhere: Discover, profiles, requests, chats and group plans. Unblock from the **Safety centre** |
| **Report** | Something unsafe or fake | Goes privately to the safety team. You can also block them at the same time |

**Unfriend** is also a button next to each person under **Requests → Connected**. Unfriend and Block always ask "Are you sure?" first.

**Nobody is ever told** that you muted, unfriended, blocked or reported them.

## 7. The Requests tab

The bell icon shows a red number when someone is waiting for your answer.

- **Received**: people who sent you a Spark or travel request. Accept or say not now.
- **Sent**: what you sent, and whether it's waiting, accepted or "not this time".
- **Connected**: everyone you can message, with a **Message** button.

## 8. Hosting a trip

You → **Host dashboard** → **Host a Group trip or a Singles trip**.

1. **Choose who it's for** (the first question, because the rules differ):
   - **Group trip**: anyone can join. You can make it mixed, women-only or men-only.
   - **Singles trip**: only single members open to dating can see or join it. It's **always mixed** and keeps everyone in **one age band (at most 12 years apart)**. You can only host a Singles trip if you're single and open to dating yourself.
2. **Day plan** (a few hours) or **Overnight trip** (1 to 7 nights): start blank or from a template.
3. Seven short steps: basics, when & where, itinerary, **who it's for**, pricing, safety, review. You can still switch between Group and Singles in "Who it's for"; switching to Singles removes women-only/men-only and narrows the age band for you.
4. **Send for review.** Once approved it appears in Discover → Trips, in the **Group trips** or **Singles trips** list.

The live preview shows exactly how guests will see the card, including "Singles only · Ages 24–34" for Singles trips. Your drafts are labelled **Group trip** or **Singles trip** on the dashboard.

## 9. Your profile ("You" tab)

- **Your photos**: 1 to 6. Choose your main photo, or remove any photo except the last one (a profile always keeps at least one).
- **Private details**: surname, workplace, area, Instagram. All optional. Only people you've connected with can see them.
- Your travel style, relationship status, and dating on or off.

## How we keep you safe

| Protection | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Verified members only** | Every member has at least one photo and has passed the selfie check before they can send any request |
| **Singles trips stay separate** | Only single members open to dating can see, join or host them. Everyone else sees Group trips only |
| **Nobody can message you first** | Chat opens only after **you** accept, or after you're both approved on the same group |
| **Private details stay private** | Surname, workplace, area and Instagram unlock only when a request is accepted |
| **No contact details in requests** | Notes with phone numbers, links, UPI IDs or money talk are blocked before sending |
| **No repeated asking** | After "Not now", that person can't send you another request |
| **Limits on requests** | One member can have at most 10 requests waiting (5 Sparks) at once, so nobody can spam everyone |
| **Dating only for singles** | Married members can't send or receive Sparks |
| **Silent decline** | Saying no never tells anyone why, and there is no "who rejected you" list |
| **Block and report everywhere** | The "…" menu on every profile and chat. Blocking hides you from each other everywhere and closes any open request |
| **Location stays private** | Rounded to about 1 km, forgotten when you close the tab, never shown to others. Others see only your city, and distances to people are rough bands |
| **Mute, unfriend, block** | Three levels of distance from anyone, all silent, all reversible from your profile or the Safety centre |
| **Safe photos** | Photos are checked and **location data (GPS) is removed** before saving. Only JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB |
| **Women-only and men-only groups** | Hidden from people they aren't for |
| **Money stays in the app** | Group seats are paid in the app and held until the plan happens. Nobody should ask you to pay them directly |
| **Trip Mode** | SOS button, evening check-ins on overnight trips, trip sharing with your emergency contacts |

**Safety tip:** for a first meeting with someone new, choose a group trip or a busy public place, and tell a friend where you're going.

## For the team: what's real and what's not yet

This is a **front-end prototype**. Everything is saved in the browser of the person using it.

| Works now | Simulated (needs a backend) |
|---|---|
| Full Spark, travel request and group join flows | Other members replying (use **Simulate accept / decline** on a profile) |
| Real photo upload, with checks and location removal | Photo moderation (nudity, minors, face match with the selfie) |
| Private details hidden or shown by connection status | Server-side checks of who may see whose details |
| All request rules (limits, no re-asking, note screening) | The same rules enforced on the server |
| Group join → host approval → payment states | Host approval, payment, SMS codes, ID and selfie checks |

### Before launch, the backend must:

1. **Enforce every rule on the server.** The rules are in `lib/asks.ts` (`askProblem`, `relationTo`, `detailsFor`). Browser checks can be bypassed.
2. **Never send private details or other members' choices to a browser** unless the two members are connected.
3. **Rate-limit** requests per member per day, on top of the "waiting" caps.
4. **Store photos privately** (signed URLs), re-check file type and size, strip metadata again, and moderate every photo.
5. **Log reports and blocks** for the safety team, with a 24-hour response target.
6. Get **legal review** of the drafted legal pages and fill in the company details.
7. **Replace the prototype sign-in**: real accounts in a database, server-checked passwords and codes, real SMS/email, and a session issued only after that. See "Prototype limits" in the README.
